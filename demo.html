⚡ What This Demonstrates (Minimum Requirement Met)
  • Verified 4D-GS Backbone: Full reproduction of CVPR 2024 paper (Wu et al.) running end-to-end.
  • Real-Time Performance: Free-viewpoint camera trajectory rendered at 80–120 FPS (vs <0.1 FPS for NeRFs).
  • Explicit 3D Deformation: Space-time HexPlane multi-resolution neural voxel planes model non-rigid motion over time.
  • The Limitation Visible Here: The model only reconstructs raw RGB colors and geometry; it has no semantic identity. You cannot select or edit the character's limbs or objects.
